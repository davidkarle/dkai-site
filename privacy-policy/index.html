Privacy Policy

David Karle, a sole proprietor doing business as DKAI ("DKAI," "I," "me," or "we"), operates the website https://dkai.io and related services that help small local businesses choose, set up, and look after AI tools.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the website, emails, calls, and text messages you exchange with us.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us when you use the website, fill out a form, email or call us, book a conversation, or opt in to text messages. This may include your name, business name, mobile phone number, email address, city or service area, and details about your business and what you want help with.

We may also collect basic technical information from your use of the website, such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, to operate and improve the site.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to you, explain and demonstrate DKAI services, schedule conversations and product demos, provide the services you ask for, send service-related and demo text messages you have opted into, and improve how we work.

SMS opt-in and text messaging

If you text DEMO, we collect the mobile number you texted from. If you check the optional SMS consent box when you submit a form, we collect the name and mobile number you entered with that consent. Checking that box is optional.

If you opt in to text messages, we use your mobile number to send the messages described in our Mobile Messaging Terms. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the website, communications, and business (for example hosting and phone/text software), and only as needed to provide our services or as required by law.

Cookies and tracking

This website is made of static pages. It does not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies, and it does not run analytics software.

The pages load typefaces from Google Fonts so the site can use its chosen fonts. We do not use that to advertise to you or to track your visit.

One page includes a short script that only opens an email on your own device when you submit an opt-in form. That script does not set a cookie and does not send the form to a server.

Data security

We take reasonable care to protect the information you share with us. Access to that information is limited to David Karle. We also rely on reputable service providers that help us run the website, email, phone, and text messages, and they handle information only as needed for that work.

We do not sell mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data, and we do not share them with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Your choices

You can opt out of DKAI text messages at any time by replying STOP. You can also email david@dkai.io to ask us to update or delete your information, subject to any records we must keep.

Contact

If you have questions, or you want us to update or delete your information, contact us at david@dkai.io or (808) 807-8838, or write to 1604 E 33rd St, Vancouver, WA 98663.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the bottom will change when we do. The current version will be posted at https://dkai.io/privacy-policy/.
