How it works

Small steps, real numbers.

  1. We talk

    A free, friendly call about how your days run and where time and money slip away. I listen. No sales pitch.

  2. I make a short plan

    One to three fixes, ranked by what they're worth to you, with a clear price before anything starts.

  3. I set it up

    I build and test everything, using tools you already pay for wherever they can do the job. You approve the wording.

  4. I keep it running

    I watch it closely after launch, send you a one-page report each month and adjust as your business changes.

Working with me

Helpful tech, without the tech talk.

You shouldn't have to become an AI expert to benefit from it. My job is to make it work in your business and to stay out of your way.

  • Plain languageNo jargon, no buzzwords. If I can't explain it simply, I won't sell it to you.

Questions

What owners ask first.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can send a text, you can use what I set up. I do the building, testing and upkeep, and I show you the few things you need to know.

Will my customers feel like they're talking to a robot?

The goal is the opposite. AI covers the moments you can't, such as a missed call or a late-night question, in wording you've approved. Anything that needs you still comes to you.

What if it gets something wrong?

You approve every message before it goes live and give me a list of things it must never say. AI assistants take details and hand off to you. They don't quote prices or make promises for you.

What does it cost?

It depends on what we set up, so the first call is free and you get a clear price before any work starts. We compare it with what the problem costs you now, using your own numbers.

I already pay for software. Do I have to switch?

Usually not. I check what your current tools can already do. Often the feature is there and just needs setting up properly.

I don't have time for a big project.

Neither do most owners. We start with one small fix, and your part is mostly answering a few questions and approving the wording.
